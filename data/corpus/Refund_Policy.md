# Return and Refund Policy 

## Return Period

Customers have 30 calendar days from the date of receipt to return an item. After this period, no returns will be accepted, except in the case of a confirmed manufacturing defect, for which the warranty period is extended to 12 months.

## Eligibility Conditions

To be eligible for a refund, the item must be:

Unused and in its original condition

Accompanied by its original packaging

Accompanied by proof of purchase (invoice or order number)

The following items are not eligible for return: personalized products, opened hygiene products, gift cards, and downloaded software.

## Refund Process

Once the returned item has been received and inspected, the customer will receive a confirmation email. The refund will be processed within 5 to 10 business days to the original payment method. Initial shipping costs are non-refundable, except in cases where the error was on our part (wrong item sent or defective product).

## Exchanges

An exchange for an item in a different size or color is possible within the same timeframe as a standard return (30 days). The first exchange per order is free of charge; subsequent exchanges will be charged at the standard shipping rate.

## International Returns

Customers outside the European Union are responsible for return shipping costs and any applicable customs fees. Refunds cover the item price only and do not include import taxes already paid upon receipt.
