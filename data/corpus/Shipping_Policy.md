# Shipping Policy

## Delivery Times

Standard delivery: 3 to 5 business days

Express delivery: 1 to 2 business days (additional fee of €9.90)

International delivery: 7 to 14 business days, depending on the destination

Delivery times are calculated from the shipping confirmation date, not the order date. Orders placed after 2:00 PM are processed on the next business day.

## Shipping Fees

Standard delivery is free for all orders over €50. For orders below this amount, a €4.90 shipping fee applies. Express delivery is always subject to a fee, regardless of the order value.

## Order Tracking

A tracking number will be sent by email as soon as the package is shipped. Real-time tracking is available through our partner carrier. In the event that a package is lost or damaged during transit, please contact customer support within 48 hours of the expected delivery date.

## Areas Not Served

Certain remote or island areas may not be covered by standard delivery. In such cases, the customer will receive an email with alternative options, such as the nearest pickup point, an extended delivery time, or cancellation with a full refund.

## Pickup Point Delivery

Orders can be delivered to one of our 3,**000** partner pickup points. Packages are held for 10 business days before being returned to the sender if they are not collected.
