# Loyalty Programme

## How Points Work

Every euro spent earns 1 loyalty point. Points are automatically credited 48 hours after the order delivery has been confirmed (rather than at the time of purchase), to account for any potential returns.

## Loyalty Tiers

Tier	Points Required	Benefits
Bronze	0	Access to private sales
Silver	**500**	+ Free express delivery
Gold	1,**500**	+ Permanent 10% discount
Platinum	5,**000**	+ Priority customer support, birthday gift

The loyalty tier is recalculated every January, based on the points accumulated over the previous rolling 12-month period.

## Using Points

**100** points are equivalent to €1 in discount, redeemable once **500** points have been accumulated. Points cannot be combined with certain exceptional promotions (sales, flash sales). Points expire 24 months after the date they were earned if they have not been used.

## Referral Programme

Referring a new customer earns the referrer **200** points and gives the referred customer a €10 discount code, valid on their first order over €30. Referral bonuses are capped at 10 successful referrals per year.

## Account Closure

If an account is closed, all accumulated points are permanently forfeited and cannot be refunded or converted into monetary value.
